## Voice & audience

Starting notes, inferred from the site copy. Edit freely.

**Who we write for**
- Founders, owners and operations heads of small and mid-size businesses who want AI to do real work inside existing workflows.
- Tech leads and founders shipping AI-generated code who worry about security.
- Industry buyers: law firm partners and office managers (immigration, personal injury, family law), real estate agents and brokers, travel agencies and membership operators.

**Tone**
- Plain, direct, practical. Evidence over hype.
- Calm confidence: show what was built and what happened, not promises.
- Short sentences. Concrete nouns (intake form, missed consult, invoice, API key).
- "We" for the company; speak to "you" as a busy owner.

**Words and habits to avoid**
- Hype and buzzwords: "revolutionary", "game-changing", "unlock", "supercharge", "synergy", "cutting-edge".
- Invented numbers, client names or testimonials. The site says "no invented numbers"; keep to that.
- Legal-advice claims for law-firm content (the site states it is software services, not legal advice).
- Emoji spam, fear-mongering, and jargon without explanation.

**Sign-off style**
- One clear next step (book a call, free scan, read the case study). No stacked CTAs.
