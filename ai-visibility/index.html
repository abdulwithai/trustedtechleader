Questions about the AI visibility diagnostic

What is an AI visibility diagnostic?

We ask real buying questions for your practice areas and city on ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity, then record whether your firm is named, how it is described, which firms appear instead, and which sources the answers cite.

Why would a law firm need to know what AI assistants say?

People now ask full questions and an assistant answers with a few names instead of a page of links. There is no ranking report for AI answers, so unless someone asks the questions you cannot know whether you are named, missing or described wrongly.

What do I receive?

A report you can read in one sitting: the full question grid by assistant, every wrong detail with where it came from, the firms named in your place, and an ordered fix list that is yours to keep.

Do you guarantee that AI assistants will mention my firm?

No. AI answers change from run to run. We report what we saw and never promise mentions, citations or rankings.
