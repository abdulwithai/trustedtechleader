Getting started

Create notes, organise them in nested folders, and add #hashtags to tag them. Use the search box for full-text search.

Locked notes

Each locked note is encrypted separately with your password. Save the recovery key shown when you lock a note: without the password or recovery key, a locked note cannot be opened by anyone.

Google Drive sync (optional)

Use Sync & Backup to connect. NotiPad only uses the hidden app-data folder in your Drive. To remove the cloud backup, open Google Drive > Settings > Manage apps > NotiPad > Delete hidden app data. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions and reconnect if sign-in fails.

Where your data is stored

Notes are stored on your PC in the app's data folder. Windows removes this data when you uninstall the app, so back up or sync first.
