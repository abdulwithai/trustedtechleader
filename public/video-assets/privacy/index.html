Video Asset Search Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026
Developer: abdulwithai (Trusted Tech Leader)
Contact: abdulrahman.connect@outlook.com

Video Asset Search ("the App", "we", "us") is a cloud application that helps video creators find visual references for their projects. When you connect your Pinterest account, the App searches Pinterest for pins, images and videos related to the video you are working on and shows them to you as inspiration and reference material. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, how long it is kept and how you can remove it.

Summary

  • The App only accesses Pinterest data after you sign in with Pinterest and approve access. We never see your Pinterest password.
  • We request read-only access. The App does not create, edit or delete pins or boards on your behalf.
  • Pinterest data is used only to show you relevant pins, images and videos inside the App.
  • We do not sell, rent or share Pinterest data for advertising, and we do not use it to train machine-learning models.
  • You can disconnect Pinterest and ask us to delete your data at any time.

Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account details you give us when you sign up for the App, such as your name and email address.
  • Project information you enter to drive a search, such as a video title, description, keywords or topics.

Information we receive from Pinterest

When you connect your Pinterest account through Pinterest's official OAuth sign-in, we receive an access token and, depending on the permissions you approve on Pinterest's consent screen, the following read-only data:

  • Basic profile information: your Pinterest username, account ID and profile image (to show which account is connected).
  • Pins and boards you can access, and pin search results: titles, descriptions, links, image and video URLs, and related metadata.

The exact permissions (scopes) are listed on Pinterest's consent screen before you approve them. We request only the scopes the App needs to search and display content.

Technical information

  • Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, time of request, errors) used to operate, secure and troubleshoot the App.

How we use information

  • To search Pinterest for pins, images and videos that match your video project and display the results to you.
  • To let you save references to pins within your project in the App. Saved items link back to the original pin on Pinterest, and Pinterest content is shown with attribution to Pinterest and its creator.
  • To keep you signed in to Pinterest so you do not have to reconnect for every search.
  • To operate, maintain, secure and improve the App.

We do not:

  • Sell or rent Pinterest data or any personal information.
  • Use Pinterest data for advertising, ad targeting or building user profiles.
  • Use Pinterest data to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • Download and redistribute Pinterest images or videos outside the App, or present Pinterest content as our own.
  • Post, edit or delete anything on your Pinterest account.

Our use of Pinterest data complies with the Pinterest Developer Guidelines.

How long we keep data

  • Pinterest access tokens are kept while your Pinterest account is connected and deleted when you disconnect.
  • Pinterest search results are cached only briefly to keep the App fast, and are refreshed from Pinterest rather than stored long term.
  • References to pins you saved in a project (pin ID and link) are kept until you remove them, delete the project or delete your account.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 90 days.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your personal data and all Pinterest data associated with it within 30 days, except where we are legally required to keep it.

How we share information

We share information only in these cases:

  • Service providers: cloud hosting and infrastructure providers that run the App on our behalf, under contracts that require them to protect the data and use it only to provide their service.
  • Pinterest: your search requests are sent to Pinterest's API to retrieve results. Pinterest handles that data under the Pinterest Privacy Policy.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or security of our users, the App or others.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Pinterest access tokens are stored on our servers, never in your browser's local storage, and access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your controls and deleting your data

  • Disconnect Pinterest inside the App. We delete your stored Pinterest access token and cached Pinterest data.
  • Revoke the App's access from Pinterest at any time: in Pinterest, go to Settings > Security and logins (or Apps), find Video Asset Search and remove it.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by emailing abdulrahman.connect@outlook.com. We respond within 30 days.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or a jurisdiction with similar laws, you also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at https://trustedtechleader.com/video-assets/privacy/. If changes are material, we will notify connected users before they take effect.
