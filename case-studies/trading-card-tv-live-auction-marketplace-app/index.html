Trading Card TV: live-stream auctions, marketplace and card trading app

A live-stream shopping, auction, marketplace and card-for-card trading app for trading-card collectors on iOS and Android.

Sector
Collectibles: live-stream commerce for trading cards
Category
Mobile apps
Scope
One public app, Trading Card TV, described from its App Store and Google Play listings, October 2026.

At a glance

Who it's for
Trading-card collectors and sellers (sports cards, Pokémon, Magic and other card games) who want to buy, sell and trade through live shows and a marketplace. Both stores rate it 18+.
Core stack
An iOS and iPadOS app (iOS 13.0 or later, 176.4 MB on the App Store) and an Android app, with live video, live chat, push notifications and a paid membership sold as an in-app purchase.
What sets it apart
One app combines live shows, auctions, a listings marketplace and member-to-member card trading. The App Store subtitle is "Shop Live With Less Fees", and the Google Play listing also positions it on lower fees.

The live app

Four of the nine screenshots on the App Store listing, captured October 2026. Each opens the listing.

01

The challenge

Card collectors already buy and sell on large live-selling platforms and general marketplaces. A new app has to give hosts and buyers a reason to move. The app's pitch is lower fees and a tool built only for cards.

It also has to handle real-time work in one place: video, chat, bidding, giveaways, payments, shipping and trust between strangers trading valuable items.

02

What is built

Trading Card TV is a mobile app from Collectible Events Group LLC, the seller named on the App Store. The listings describe four parts: live shows, a marketplace, built-in card-for-card trading and seller tools.

Hosts go live to sell. Viewers bid, chat and enter giveaways. Sellers can also list cards in the marketplace, where buyers can pay a set price or win an auction. A premium membership, sold in the app for $129.99 a month on the App Store, unlocks trading and, per the screenshots, no platform fees.

03

Key features

From the store descriptions, screenshots and release notes, October 2026.

  • 01

    Live shows and breaks

    Hosts go live and sell cards in real time. Viewers bid, chat and join. A "break" (a live opening of sealed card packs where viewers buy a spot) is shown with a spot counter and a bid bar.

    The listings say hosts can add products and giveaways during a stream.

  • 02

    Auctions and giveaways

    Release notes mention sequential bidding, sellers pre-pinning items, pinning breaks in an auction, and giveaways with customisable durations and a timer.

    The App Store screenshots promote free giveaways.

  • 03

    Marketplace

    A browse-and-buy catalogue next to the live shows. The Google Play text says it can be filtered by sport, set and rarity, and a release note mentions a Graded filter.

    Buy instantly or win an auction, per the App Store text.

  • 04

    Snap To List card scanning

    Version 1.1.15 added a camera scan that, per its note, recognises a card and fills in the name, set, rarity and condition for a new listing.

    Covers Pokémon, Magic and sports cards, per the note.

  • 05

    Card-for-card trading

    A trade screen lets a member offer cards to another member. Trading is described as card-for-card with no extra fees, and as a feature for verified premium members.

  • 06

    Wallet, shipping and reviews

    Screenshots show a Wallet button and a payout screen. Release notes add in-person shipping and delivery, PWE shipping for breaks (a plain white envelope, the cheap way to post a single card) and seller reviews.

    Also listed: push notifications, stream zoom and a Creator's Lounge.

Business value

What the product enables.

  • Gives a niche community one place to watch, bid, buy, list and trade, instead of stitching together several tools.
  • Snap To List fills a listing from a camera scan.
  • Seller reviews, verified members and a wallet and payout flow.
  • Sells a paid membership inside the app, tying trading and fee benefits to a recurring plan.
